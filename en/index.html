The transformation

From a photo taken in the shop to the shop window

  1. 01The photo, taken in the shopOn a hanger, held in a hand or laid on a table: a simple phone photo.
  2. 02Cut out to the pixelThe background fades away. The garment stays exactly the same: same colours, same fabric.
  3. 03In the lightThe piece steps into our set: a limewashed wall and the Tigzirt sun.

The fitting

You choose, they try it on.

Elle and Lui, our articulated wooden mannequins, have tried on every piece in the shop, in four poses. Everything is ready in advance: nothing is recalculated, and the piece stays the one in the photo.
